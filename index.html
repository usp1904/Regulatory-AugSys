Regulatory Delivery Assurance
MARAS is a regulated business value engine
MARAS is evaluated as a regulator-defensible delivery layer: source truth first, then business requirement, control intent, risk, PBI, test obligation, evidence and inspection readiness. Jira work items are only one export from the package.
1. Source of Truth Validation
Official source, license posture, and usage rule
Production rule: every ingested regulatory document must carry official URL, authority, document class, effective date, capture date, file hash, license tag, and SME approval before it is allowed to drive generated outputs. Browser ingestion now hashes files and parses TXT/CSV/JSON; missing fields hold the file. Live official re-validation and malware scanning still require a connected ingestion service.
